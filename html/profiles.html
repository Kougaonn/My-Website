Hobbes

Hobbes is an orange tabby. The oldest cat of the bunch at 16 years old. Prominent features of this creature are his size and weight, being the largest of all the cats. Do not be decieved by his lazy temperament, for this beast has demonstrated immense power, having won multiple fights with great beasts in his time, such as dogs.

Leroy

Leroy is a black shorthair cat. He is 13 years old, and is a very social animal. His hobbies include meowing at people, eating immense amounts of food at once, and laying ontop of unsuspecting victims. He and Blazer are brothers, both being very close to eachother. They are known to play wrestle at odd hours of the night, sometimes making great amounts of commotion in the process. He can be told apart from his brother by his larger body and big nose.

Blazer

Blazer, like his brother, is a black shorthair cat. He is also 13 years old. He is not nearly as social as his brother is, but likes to run about the house to find new hiding places. You will often find this beast hiding within cardboard boxes, staring at you with his green eyes from the darkness. He is the only cat that doesn't mind being picked up.
