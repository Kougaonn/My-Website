About Us

This site is devoted to the cats my brother and I have owned over the years. We had owned Hobbes ever since childhood, and were given ownership over him as we both moved out to live on our own. Each of these cats have given us a great deal of joy over these past few years. We often enjoy taking pictures of our cats, whether it be them relaxing or making silly faces/poses. We often shared these images with our friends and family. They had consistently been a source of joy in our lives and we hope they can be so for you as well!

Thank you so much for stopping by to take a look at our creatures!
